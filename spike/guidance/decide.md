# Decide

Resolve the active Change from durable evidence only.

- Verify every Ticket has a Report and operational cleanup is healthy.
- For landing, verify the current Candidate has an exact approving review and the Change base still equals the Goal integrated revision.
- Reject only with an exact rejecting review; abandon with a clear operator-facing statement.
- Do not treat passing tests, planner prose, terminal output, or worker status as approval.

Record exactly one explicit land, reject, or abandon decision. Never reopen a resolved Change.
