# Goal

Define the operator-approved outcome before planning work.

- Record one observable outcome, constraints, expected evidence, and the operator's exact approval statement.
- Ensure the outcome can be planned as independently safe, coherent Changes rather than one broad implementation.
- Each Change should address one primary behavior or risk seam and be fully implementable and verifiable in one fresh implementer session.
- Keep deferred ideas and speculative architecture outside the Goal.

Goal creation is complete only after the operator explicitly approves the recorded outcome and constraints. Never invent or infer approval.
